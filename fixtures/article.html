The Future of Local Translation

Translation models are becoming smaller, faster, and more capable. A few years ago, running a useful translation model locally required a powerful GPU. Today, a small quantized model can run on an ordinary laptop.

For web pages, the key is not the model size but the engineering around it: splitting paragraphs, batching requests, caching results, and rendering translations without breaking the layout.
